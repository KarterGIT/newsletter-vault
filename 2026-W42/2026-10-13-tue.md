## Social impact: data centers
- [ ] keep · **US power regulator approves data-center tracking, rejects forced grid disconnections**: Reuters, October 12, 2026 `[anchor]`
  - FERC allowed PJM Interconnection, a grid operator serving about 67 million people, to begin building a registry of large data centers and their power needs.
  - The regulator rejected PJM's proposed temporary power shutoffs for data centers during periods of heavy demand. PJM may submit a revised proposal.
  - PJM expects its data-center demand to reach about 29 gigawatts by 2030, roughly three times its earlier level; that is a **forecast**, not present consumption.
  - Jargon: FERC = US Federal Energy Regulatory Commission; grid curtailment = temporarily limiting electricity service.
  - https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-panel-approves-pjms-registry-data-centers-rejects-plan-curtail-power-them-2026-10-12/
- [ ] keep · **Rising AI power demand puts transmission upgrades in focus**: Reuters, October 13, 2026 `[anchor]`
  - Reuters describes how expanding data centers and bottlenecks on high-voltage lines can raise congestion costs and delay new electric generation.
  - PJM had recommended $12.2 billion in additional transmission improvements in February, mainly to address grid reliability.
  - A modeled S&P Global Energy study, supported by National Grid and the Electricity Customer Alliance, estimated $2.30 in potential PJM customer benefits per $1 invested; these are **model estimates**, not realized savings.
  - Jargon: transmission = high-voltage lines moving electricity across long distances; congestion = insufficient grid capacity to deliver power where it is needed.
  - https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/rising-ai-demand-boosts-case-new-us-trunk-power-lines--reeii-2026-10-13/
- [ ] keep · **Federal-land data centers fuel debate in Nevada's governor's race**: Associated Press, October 2026 (reviewed October 13) `[discovered]`
  - AP reports that a proposed Boulder City-area data center moved toward federal land after local opposition; the project has been held up by a judge over an inadequate earlier environmental review.
  - Republican Gov. Joe Lombardo and Democratic nominee Aaron Ford differ on projects using public land, but both discuss guardrails such as utility cost responsibility and environmental limits.
  - The reporting documents local concerns about land, water and utility costs; those concerns do not themselves establish that a specific facility has caused those harms.
  - Jargon: public land = land owned by government; environmental review = assessment of possible environmental effects before a project proceeds.
  - https://apnews.com/article/nevada-data-centers-public-lands-governors-race-777894300ca0612493a4991da5f30308
