## Technical: hardware

- [ ] keep · **Cisco raises its fiscal 2027 outlook on demand for AI networking**: Reuters, November 12, 2026.
  - Cisco reported first-quarter revenue of $18.5 billion, above the $18.14 billion LSEG analyst estimate. It raised its fiscal 2027 total-revenue forecast to $74.8–$75.8 billion from $72.2–$73.4 billion.
  - Cisco also lifted its forecast for hyperscaler AI-infrastructure revenue to $9 billion from $7.5 billion. These are management projections, not completed sales. Network switches and routers connect processors across AI data centers.
  - [Original Reuters earnings report](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/cisco-raises-annual-revenue-forecast-strong-ai-networking-demand-2026-11-12/)

- [ ] keep · **AMD plans an undisclosed equity investment in PsiQuantum**: Reuters, November 12, 2026.
  - The companies say they will jointly design infrastructure connecting quantum computing with high-performance computing and AI. AMD will provide processors, accelerators and software, while PsiQuantum contributes silicon-photonics technology.
  - The investment amount was not disclosed. Claims about improved drug-research simulations are company statements, not independent clinical evidence; utility-scale quantum computing remains a development goal.
  - [Original Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/amd-invest-psiquantum-advance-quantum-computing-infrastructure-2026-11-12/)

## China and open weights

- [ ] keep · **Reuters traces more than $3 billion of Chinese computing projects designed around restricted Nvidia chips**: Reuters, November 13, 2026.
  - A review of more than 150 procurement notices, investment plans, contracts and corporate disclosures identified projects and equipment agreements exceeding 23 billion yuan (approximately $3.2 billion).
  - This is NOT $3.2 billion in Nvidia chip purchases: the total includes planned and completed projects and associated buildings and power infrastructure. Reuters could not establish how all restricted processors reached users or whether chips arrived at some proposed sites.
  - The investigation expands on Wednesday's university procurement story rather than duplicating it; the editorial angle is the broader infrastructure pipeline and export-control enforcement.
  - [Original Reuters investigation](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/huang-exit-us-scrutiny-imperil-3-billion-chinese-ai-projects-reliant-nvidia-2026-11-13/)

## Regulation and politics

- [ ] keep · **US AI task force considers incident-reporting standards and cyber information sharing**: Reuters, published November 13, 2026, covering a November 12 meeting.
  - An administration spokesperson said advisers discussed encouraging banks, utilities and other critical-infrastructure providers to report AI-enabled attacks and vulnerabilities, and national standards for AI developers to disclose safety incidents.
  - No binding reporting standard was enacted at this meeting. The existing 2015 Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act is scheduled to expire in December, creating a separate legislative deadline.
  - [Original Reuters reporting](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trumps-ai-task-force-met-thursday-discussed-ai-powered-cyberattacks-2026-11-13/)

- [ ] keep · **Nonprofits seek a court order restricting frontier AI development**: Reuters, November 12, 2026.
  - Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and other nonprofits sued OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX's AI division in San Francisco, invoking California unfair-business-practices law.
  - The complaint asks the court to halt further development of advanced models; allegations of public nuisance and catastrophic risks are plaintiffs' claims. No injunction or finding of liability was reported.
  - [Original Reuters legal reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-google-anthropic-hit-with-lawsuit-seeking-halt-frontier-ai-development-2026-11-12/)

- [ ] keep · **EU leaders gather for first dedicated AI summit**: Reuters, November 13, 2026.
  - The Dublin agenda includes AI competitiveness, funding, regulation and risk, with Mistral chief Arthur Mensch and researcher Geoffrey Hinton due to brief leaders.
  - Reuters reports that the seven proposed European AI gigafactories have not fully advanced amid budget disputes. The meeting is a discussion, not an approved new financing package or final regulation.
  - [Original Reuters summit preview](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/eu-leaders-seek-tackle-challenges-first-ever-ai-summit-2026-11-13/)
