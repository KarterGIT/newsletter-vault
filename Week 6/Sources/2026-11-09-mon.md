## Technical: frontier models

- [ ] keep · **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 released**: Anthropic, November 9, 2026.
  - Anthropic describes Fable as broadly available with stricter safeguards; access to Mythos, the same underlying model with different safeguards, is limited to verified cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations.
  - Anthropic quotes $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens and says generation can be up to 30% faster than 5.1. Those speed and benchmark comparisons are developer claims, not independently validated.
  - Anthropic also reports experiments in code migration, mathematical proof formalization, and protein modeling; do not present experimental results as broadly reproduced scientific discoveries.
  - [Original developer announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) · [Independent launch report, Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/anthropic-unveils-new-fable-mythos-models-competition-heats-up-ahead-ipo-2026-11-09/)

## Technical: hardware

- [ ] keep · **Google unveils 8tx and 8i Max TPU variants**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - The 8tx is aimed at protected computation for government and regulated industries, pairing Google TPUs with AMD processors and hardware-level encryption; Google's claimed speed gains are not independently established in this report.
  - The 8i Max packages two chips together for memory-heavy workloads such as coding, reasoning and drug discovery. TPU = tensor processing unit, a chip designed for machine-learning computation.
  - [Original Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-to-introduce-two-variants-its-in-house-chips-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-11-09/)

- [ ] keep · **AI electricity demand sends operators into the used-turbine market**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - Reuters reports that developers are sourcing refurbished gas turbines and engines adapted from aviation because new equipment can take years to arrive.
  - Wood Mackenzie estimates global orders above 100 gigawatts versus annual manufacturing capacity of roughly 60–70 GW; this is an analyst estimate, not a measured universal shortage.
  - [Original Reuters reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/ai-power-rush-sends-data-centres-hunting-used-turbines-jet-engines-2026-11-09/)

## Jobs

- [ ] keep · **Australian central banker sees limited economy-wide AI job and productivity effects so far**: Reuters, published November 8 UTC; remarks delivered November 9 in Sydney.
  - Reserve Bank of Australia Assistant Governor Sarah Hunter said evidence of broad productivity gains and widespread job losses remains limited, while noting tentative changes in postings and outcomes for more AI-exposed occupations, particularly among younger workers.
  - This assessment concerns Australia and is not evidence that AI has had no labor effects elsewhere.
  - [Original Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/rbas-hunter-says-ai-has-yet-deliver-productivity-boost-trigger-job-losses-2026-11-08/)

- [ ] keep · **Disney and Adobe agree to develop private generative models for Disney workflows**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - The companies plan models trained on Disney intellectual property through Adobe Firefly Foundry for parks, studio ideation and sports media. Disney says existing image-generation tools have sped up creative iterations.
  - A Disney executive said the tools are not intended to replace Imagineering jobs; that is a company position, not a guarantee about employment outcomes.
  - [Original Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/disney-adobe-co-develop-custom-ai-models-tailored-media-giant-2026-11-09/)

## Social impact: data centers

- [ ] keep · **Banks and investors become more selective on AI debt**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - Reuters interviewed finance and lending sources who reported increasing scrutiny of borrowers, contracts and downside cash-flow scenarios after a market selloff.
  - Goldman Sachs Research forecasts global hyperscaler debt issuance of $420 billion in 2027 versus an estimated $250 billion in fiscal 2026. Both figures are forecasts/estimates, not actual issuance.
  - [Original Reuters investigation](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/banks-investors-grow-wary-ai-debt-spooked-by-market-selloff-2026-11-09/)

- [ ] keep · **Norway pauses new data-center grid reservations pending licensing rules**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - Grid operator Statnett temporarily stopped accepting new connection reservations while the government prepares a licensing system prioritizing critical infrastructure and domestic needs.
  - Norway's energy minister said data centers represented 75% of 26,000 MW in connection requests received this year; requested capacity is not built capacity.
  - [Original Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/norways-statnett-pauses-new-grid-capacity-data-centres-protect-power-system-2026-11-09/)

## Regulation and politics

- [ ] keep · **US Senate weighs data-center grid-cost provisions in a broader permitting bill**: Reuters, November 9, 2026.
  - The proposed Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act would accelerate energy infrastructure approvals and require large data-center operators to cover more related grid upgrade costs.
  - The bill was due for Senate debate on November 9. It has not been enacted; passage remains uncertain and some environmental groups and lawmakers oppose provisions.
  - [Original Reuters reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-congress-gets-first-post-blue-wave-crack-data-centers-with-permitting-bill-2026-11-09/)
