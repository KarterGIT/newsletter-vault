## China and open weights

- [ ] keep · **Reuters finds blacklisted Chinese university procured Nvidia H200 chips**: Reuters, November 11, 2026.
  - Reuters reviewed procurement records showing Northwestern Polytechnical University bought two H200 chips in October for 874,700 yuan from a domestic supplier and contracted for additional computing access. Its review of 26 records points to wider acquisition of restricted chips or services. Reuters could not establish where all chips originated or servers operated. Do not assume Nvidia directly supplied the university.
  - [Original report](https://www.reuters.com/world/pla-linked-chinese-university-bought-nvidia-h200-chips-despite-us-blacklist-2026-11-11/)
