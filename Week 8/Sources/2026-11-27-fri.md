## Technical: frontier models and engineering

### Anthropic uses Claude to migrate its production API to Rust
- **Publisher/date:** Anthropic, November 25, 2026.
- Anthropic reports approximately 70% lower CPU consumption per API request and a 20% reduction in median time to first token after the migration. Engineers compared the new implementation with live production behavior; an audit of ported tests found over 100 weakened assertions. The figures are internal measurements, not independent benchmarks. Editorial angle: verification can be harder than generating code, particularly for live systems.
- [Original source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust)

## China and open weights

### Researchers identify MiniMax in data-exposure investigation
- **Publisher/date:** Reuters, November 26, 2026.
- Dragos researcher HD Moore reported that private links submitted through MiniMax's hosted service were later accessed despite retention opt-outs. MiniMax acknowledged collecting links for security screening, said it deleted them, and reported no evidence of third-party disclosure of conversations in its review. Atlas Cloud stopped forwarding requests. The identity of the parties accessing links remains unknown. Editorial angle: distinguish hosted service privacy from locally operated model weights.
- [Original source](https://www.reuters.com/world/data-chinese-ai-firm-minimax-accessed-by-unidentified-parties-researcher-says-2026-11-26/)

## Regulation and politics

### Bank of England warns about interconnected AI financing and operational risks
- **Publisher/date:** Bank of England, November 26, 2026.
- The central bank's new Financial Stability Report identifies increasingly complex AI financing arrangements as a potential channel for stress to spread between markets. Shared technology providers and increasingly capable agents also raise operational and cybersecurity concerns. The report describes the UK financial system as resilient so far. This is a risk assessment, not a prediction of an imminent crash. Distinct new official report, following earlier US central bank commentary.
- [Original source](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/november-2026)

- **Publisher/date:** OpenAI, November 23, 2026.
