## Social impact: math and science

### OpenAI reports protein-design progress in GPT-6
- **Publisher/date:** OpenAI, November 24, 2026.
- The developer's preprint reports that general-purpose models can propose protein shapes and sequences. Laboratory work with Onava found binding evidence across five targets. Specialized biology tools still assisted candidate preparation and screening; the experiments do not establish a fully independent design pipeline or therapeutic efficacy. Whole-protein structure prediction remains substantially behind specialist systems. Editorial angle: distinguish a useful research component from a validated medicine.
- [Original source](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

- The agreement covers employees in India, Hungary and Malaysia, with transfers planned for 2027 subject to conditions. BP retains responsibility for data and standards. ## Social impact: data centers

### FCC authorizes orbital computing constellation
- **Publisher/date:** Reuters, November 25, 2026.
- The authorization covers up to one million SpaceX Starmind satellites. License milestones require deployment to begin by November 2033, with 100,000 by November 2035 and half the authorized number by November 2038. Authorization is not deployment. SpaceX's projected energy savings are company claims; launch economics remain uncertain. Editorial angle: infrastructure ambitions versus engineering feasibility. FCC direct statement returned HTTP 403; corroborating reporting: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/25/spacex-datacenter-satellites-approval
- [Original source](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-authorizes-spacex-deploy-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space-2026-11-25/)

### Enverus compares AI water consumption with other spending
- **Publisher/date:** Reuters, November 24, 2026.
- Enverus estimates 1.9 gallons of water consumption for $25 in monthly AI spending, including electricity generation. Its analysis attributes about 90% of model-operation water consumption to power plants. These are study estimates normalized by spending, not universal measurements per prompt. Editorial angle: price-based comparisons cannot settle local water scarcity or total industry demand.
- [Original source](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-consumes-less-water-per-dollar-spent-than-almond-milk-or-golf-enverus-report-2026-11-24/)

## Enterprise adoption and cybersecurity

### Retailers face holiday cyber risks
- **Publisher/date:** Reuters, November 25, 2026.
- Insurer CFC's claims data indicate ransomware frequency in retail, leisure and hospitality is 50% above the monthly average in November. CFC reports cyber coverage for 43% of small and midsize retailers. Security specialists describe AI accelerating attacks as well as defense. The seasonal statistic does not isolate AI's causal contribution. Editorial angle: operational resilience during peak trading.
- [Original source](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/retailers-face-rising-ai-cyberattack-threat-ahead-black-friday-many-uninsured-2026-11-25/)

### Nio outlines AI deployment and margin ambitions
- **Publisher/date:** Reuters, November 25, 2026.
- Nio says it has deployed 7,500 agents across functions including manufacturing, research, logistics and sales. CEO William Li estimates AI could eventually add ten percentage points to gross margin. Neither agent effectiveness nor the projected margin improvement has been independently established. Editorial angle: a concrete enterprise deployment claim paired with a measurable but unproven financial target.
- [Original source](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nio-says-ai-use-could-lift-gross-margin-by-10-percentage-points-2026-11-25/)
