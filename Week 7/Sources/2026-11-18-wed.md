## Technical: hardware and China
- [ ] keep · **Nvidia stops licensed Hopper shipments to China**: Reuters, November 18, 2026.
  - Interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia sold $2.4 billion in licensed Hopper-generation data-center products to China in its quarter ending October 25, but no further licensed Hopper shipments are included in its outlook. The family includes H200 chips.
  - Reuters says the company did not specify why shipments stopped or whether the pause is permanent. Do not label this a permanent ban.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/nvidia-stops-shipping-us-approved-ai-chips-china-2026-11-18/)
- [ ] keep · **Tencent distinguishes inference from training under new chip rules**: Reuters, November 18, 2026.
  - Tencent executives said new US restrictions on overseas remote access to advanced chips are unlikely to materially hurt current cloud revenue, because much of that business runs inference (using an already-trained model).
  - President Martin Lau said the same restrictions may slow development of Tencent's next model, which requires training compute. Tencent's third-quarter revenue grew 8% to 207.3 billion yuan, below the cited 210.8 billion yuan consensus estimate. These are company comments and reported earnings, not independently proven future effects.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/technology/tencent-q3-revenue-growth-slows-8-misses-estimates-2026-11-18/)

## China and open weights
- [ ] keep · **Reuters traces model distillation into Chinese industry**: Reuters investigation, November 17, 2026.
  - Reuters examined more than 300 Chinese papers, patents, disclosures, job notices and government records documenting ways US model outputs are used to help train smaller domestic models.
  - *Distillation* transfers selected behaviors or skills from a larger model into a smaller one, often using generated examples. It is not inherently illegal, does not reproduce the full original system and does not eliminate compute requirements. Distinguish documented general practices from allegations against any particular firm.
  - [Original investigation](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-taps-us-ai-models-train-homegrown-rivals-chip-curbs-bite-2026-11-17/)

## Social impact: data centers
- [ ] keep · **Malaysia tells data-center operators to comply with US chip controls**: Reuters, November 18, 2026.
  - Trade Minister Johari Abdul Ghani said operators should comply with newly announced US advanced-chip restrictions and Malaysia would seek clarification on licensing and third-party services.
  - Malaysia has approved 117 data-center projects, according to the minister's remarks cited by Reuters. He said Malaysia had not yet received evidence that local tenants had illegally re-exported chips; do not imply violations were established.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/malaysia-says-data-centres-must-comply-with-us-ai-chip-rules-2026-11-18/)
- [ ] keep · **AI climate pledge struggles to attract major US hyperscalers**: Reuters, November 17, 2026.
  - At COP31 in Antalya, the voluntary Antalya AI Pledge called for cleaner energy, efficiency, emissions tracking and broader AI access. Reuters reported 90 initial endorsements, with Alibaba the only major technology company among signatories at the time.
  - Germany, Brazil and South Korea were among government signatories. Meta, Microsoft, Google and Nvidia had not responded to Reuters' comment requests; their absence from the pledge is not proof they oppose its goals.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/cop31-ai-pledge-fails-lure-us-big-tech-2026-11-17/)

## Regulation and politics
- [ ] keep · **White House weighs Chinese-model disclosure requirement**: Reuters exclusive, November 18, 2026.
  - Three sources told Reuters that Treasury and White House science-policy officials discussed requiring US companies to publicly identify their use of Chinese AI models. Reuters reported Airbnb's limited use of Qwen, while Airbnb emphasized that its AI activity overwhelmingly uses US-origin models.
  - The idea is preliminary: no formal rule, binding requirement or implementation timetable was confirmed. Editorially distinguish this from Monday's multinational AI supply-chain declaration.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-administration-weighs-forcing-us-firms-disclose-chinese-ai-use-2026-11-18/)
- [ ] keep · **Bipartisan senators call for stronger AI cyberdefenses**: Reuters, November 18, 2026.
  - Senators Maria Cantwell and Mike Rounds wrote to White House task-force chair Jay Clayton urging defensive AI tools and stronger standards for autonomous agents, citing recent software and cybersecurity incidents.
  - This is a congressional appeal, not enacted federal security standards. Allegations about individual AI systems should remain attributed to the letter and underlying reports.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-senators-warn-white-house-that-ai-enabled-cyber-threats-are-outpacing-2026-11-18/)
- [ ] keep · **Justice Department AI misuse findings illustrate professional risks**: Reuters, November 18, 2026.
  - The DOJ Office of Professional Responsibility described two cases: one prosecutor filed a document with fabricated quotations and misrepresented AI involvement to a judge; another lawyer uploaded restricted work materials to a public AI platform despite restrictions.
  - The findings were referred to the Professional Misconduct Review Unit for possible discipline. Names and any final disciplinary outcomes were not disclosed. This is an accountability and workplace adoption story, not a finding that all AI-assisted legal work is improper.
  - [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/two-us-justice-department-lawyers-face-misconduct-inquiries-over-ai-use-2026-11-18/)
