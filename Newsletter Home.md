## How to use this vault
Open a week to see its research files. Each research note includes original publication links, concise facts and terminology. Check `[x] keep` next to news you want in the weekly draft.

The Sunday newsletter is saved as a draft and linked from its week page after it has been created. Its articles will also link directly to their supporting research notes.

The `_system/` folder contains templates and research rules. Those operational files are hidden from the default Graph View for readability.

*Research is verified before selection. A newsletter remains a draft until approved by a human editor.*
