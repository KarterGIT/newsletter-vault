## Technical: frontier models

- [ ] keep · **Tencent releases Hy4 with restricted offensive cyber training**: Reuters, November 4. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/tencent-rolls-out-ai-model-trained-with-limited-cybersecurity-data-amid-hacking-2026-11-04/).
  - Tencent says it limited cybersecurity training to defensive flaw identification and fixes, excluding material for building attack tools or intruding into systems.
  - Its own internal safety tests found no increased cyberattack assistance or meaningful biological misuse capability; these are company-reported tests, not independently reproduced results.
  - Tencent says model weights and a safety report will be released later, so do not call it already open-weight.

## Technical: hardware

- [ ] keep · **AMD data-center revenue jumps 97% as AI chip demand grows**: Reuters, November 3. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/amd-forecasts-quarterly-revenue-above-estimates-data-center-growth-accelerates-2026-11-03/).
  - AMD reported a 97% year-over-year increase in third-quarter data-center revenue and projected about $17 billion in fourth-quarter company revenue, plus or minus 3%.
  - CEO Lisa Su forecast more than 80% overall revenue growth in 2027, driven heavily by data-center chips. This is management guidance, not achieved growth.
  - The results are a fresh quarterly update, distinct from Monday's infrastructure borrowing and financing stories.

- [ ] keep · **AI chip supply agreements also carry shareholder dilution risks**: Reuters, November 4. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/amds-openai-meta-chip-deals-trigger-1-billion-quarterly-charge-2026-11-04/).
  - Reuters reports AMD recorded a $1.07 billion non-cash third-quarter charge associated with warrants granted to OpenAI and Meta.
  - The warrants depend on commercial, shipment and share-price milestones; potential future dilution is not the same as shares already issued.
  - Editorial angle: AI hardware deals can combine chip sales and equity incentives, complicating interpretation of headline revenues.

## Regulation and politics

- [ ] keep · **US bank regulator publishes its first AI examination guidance**: Reuters, November 4. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/us-bank-regulator-issues-first-ever-ai-guidance-examiners-2026-11-04/).
  - The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency told examiners to evaluate how banks use AI under existing risk principles rather than treat AI as a standalone risk category.
  - A relatively simple agent able to initiate financial transactions may be riskier than a powerful model used internally, because consequences and autonomy matter.
  - The guidance is supervisory and nonbinding, not a new federal law.

- [ ] keep · **EU court adviser says Google's Gemini responses may infringe publishers' rights**: Reuters, November 3. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/world/googles-use-press-by-ai-chatbot-may-infringe-copyright-rules-eu-court-adviser-2026-11-03/).
  - An adviser to the EU's highest court argued that using press articles in Gemini answers may breach copyright rules.
  - This is an advocate/adviser's legal opinion, not the court's final judgment; avoid presenting liability as established.

- [ ] keep · **India warns financial institutions about AI-assisted attacks**: Reuters, November 4. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/india-cybercrime-agency-warns-financial-firms-ai-attacks-2026-11-04/).
  - India's I4C cybercrime coordination agency warned that attackers can exploit unpatched or misconfigured systems and then target payment interfaces and automated fund disbursements at machine speed.
  - It recommended transaction limits, beneficiary checks and monitoring for unusually fast transactions alongside CERT-In safeguards.
  - This is a new advisory, distinct from earlier reporting about AI agents accessing live Indian systems.

- [ ] keep · **European technology groups plan sovereign AI compute partnership**: Reuters, November 3. [Original reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/schwarz-digits-bull-partner-up-build-european-ai-computing-services-2026-11-03/).
  - Schwarz Digits and supercomputer maker Bull signed a preliminary agreement to develop AI computing services under European jurisdiction.
  - A preliminary agreement is not evidence that the planned computing services are already operational.
