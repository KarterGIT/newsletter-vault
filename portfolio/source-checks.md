## Editorial decisions

- The historical [Week 5 draft](../Week%205/Newsletter%20Draft.md) remains unchanged.
- Reuters pages covering packaging, USDA, financial commitments, and other stories could not be opened directly during this fresh check. Those sections were omitted from the featured edition. A failed retrieval does not establish that the underlying reporting is incorrect.
- Accessible primary announcements replaced secondary reporting for the retained AMD, HSBC, and OCC stories.
- The sample separates source-backed statements from editorial interpretation and distinguishes future plans from completed outcomes.
- No interviews, independent benchmark replications, audience metrics, client results, or time savings are claimed.
- The PDF and Markdown feature the same newsletter text. The PDF adds a project overview and layout; operational YAML and hidden editorial comments are excluded.
